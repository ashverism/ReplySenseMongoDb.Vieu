Business Context

Our Understanding of Your Business

Utilities don't need another data lake. They need to connect heterogeneous systems to shared models without disrupting the systems that run the grid. EU requirements for data sovereignty and interoperability are exposing gaps in legacy systems' ability to support shared data models, and traditional alignment still relies on manual schema analysis, scarce domain expertise and repeated mapping. Semantic Kernel is built for that gap: AI-assisted semantic governance that keeps domain experts in control of validation and approval.

Semantic Kernel · The offer

A design-time governance platform that combines technical metadata, business knowledge and selected target models. AI-assisted workflows discover source structures, run semantic interviews and propose governed mappings and transformation logic, while domain experts keep validation and approval control. The platform creates reusable libraries or microservice packages with tests, documentation, versioning and source-to-target traceability. Organizations standardize data without replacing the internal models their operations depend on.
